# Privacy Policy for 2000s Toon Crosswords

**Last Updated: October 29, 2026**

## 1. Overview
"2000s Toon Crosswords" (the "Application") is developed as a premium, paid, 100% offline word puzzle game celebrating animated television history from the 2000s era. We believe in absolute privacy: the Application does not collect, transmit, store externally, or share any personal data whatsoever.

## 2. Zero Data Collection
* **No Personal Data Collected**: The Application does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, biometric data, or device identifiers.
* **No Network Permissions**: The Application does not request or include `android.permission.INTERNET` in its manifest. The application has zero physical ability to connect to the internet, servers, or cloud networks.
* **No Analytics or Trackers**: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, crash reporting SDKs, telemetry libraries, or any third-party tracking software.
* **No Advertising**: The Application is 100% ad-free. No advertising identifiers (AAID) are accessed.
* **No User Accounts**: All gameplay, progression, coin balances, and unlocked Trophy Dex cards are stored strictly on your local device.

## 3. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & GDPR Compliance
Because the Application does not collect, store, or transmit any data from any user (children or adults), it fully complies with:
- The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- The General Data Protection Regulation (GDR / UK GDPR)
- Google Play Families Policy Requirements

## 4. Local Device Storage
The Application saves puzzle completion states, coin economy balances, daily streak counters, and preferences (sound/haptics) locally on your device in private internal storage. This data is never transmitted externally. You can clear this data at any time via the in-app "Reset All Progress" button in Settings or by clearing application data through Android device settings.
