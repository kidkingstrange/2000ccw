Privacy Policy for 2000s Toon Crosswords

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

2000s Toon Crosswords (the "Application") is a premium, paid, 100% offline word puzzle game celebrating the history of animated television from the 2000s era. We believe in uncompromising privacy: this application does not collect, transmit, store externally, or share any personal data whatsoever.

2. Zero Data Collection Architecture

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, biometric data, or device identifiers.
  • Zero Internet Permissions: The Application does not declare or request the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. The app has zero physical capability to connect to servers, telemetry endpoints, or cloud services.
  • No Tracking & No Analytics: We do not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, telemetry SDKs, crash reporting tools, or third-party ad networks.
  • No Advertisements: The Application is completely ad-free. No Google Advertising ID (AAID) is accessed.
  • No Accounts Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any credentials to play.

3. Local Storage on Device

All game progress, coin balances, level completions, and unlocked Trophy Dex cards are saved strictly on your local device in sandboxed private storage. This data is never sent over any network. You can clear this data at any time via the in-app "Reset All Progress" button or through Android App Settings.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because the Application does not collect or transmit any data from any user (children or adults), it fully complies with:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR)
  • Google Play Families Policy Requirements

5. Third-Party Services

The Application contains zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), external libraries with networking capabilities, or tracking beacons.
